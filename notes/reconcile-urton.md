# Reconciliation with Urton (2017), 2026-11-18

Source: Gary Urton, *La historia inka en nudos* (Spanish edition of *Inka History in Knots*, University of Texas Press 2017), chapter 12, "Khipus de una «revisita» colonial al valle de Santa", pp. 255-282, and Appendix B (all 133 six-cord groups, positions 1 to 6, with colors). Local copy, not committed.

## How Urton gets 367
- Position 1 values of the 133 groups add up to **359** (p. 267 of the Spanish edition).
- Two position-1 cords are broken: UR90 #45 (neighbours read 4 or 5) and UR89 #171 (neighbours read 4). Imputing them gives 359 + 4/5 + 4 = **367/8**, the revisita total.
- Appendix B counts every other zero as zero, for example six all-zero groups on UR91. So "367" depends on imputing two of the fourteen zero first cords.

## The 381/86 moiety totals
- The Spanish edition gives no moiety totals. FitzPatrick (2024, note 14) cites Urton (2017: 229, English edition) for verso "381 or 382" and recto 86.
- If recto is 86 and the total is 367/8, verso must be **281/282**. "381 or 382" is most likely a misprint for 281 or 282 (the "or" mirrors Urton's 367/8). Not confirmed: needs the English edition, p. 229.

## Exploratory anomaly (not a claim)
On UR90, Appendix B position 2 rises down the first band: 15, 22, 28, 34, 38, 46, 52, 59. Position 4 also rises: 12, 16, 32, 41, 41, 67, 78, 85. That looks like running totals inside a band. Worth its own PREREG across all Santa khipus.
